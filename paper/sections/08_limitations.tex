\section*{Limitations}

\begin{itemize}[nosep]
\item The published-table decomposition (\S\ref{sec:decomposition}) covers the
  32 pairs of one study under one harness, and the first-party replication
  (\S\ref{sec:firstparty}) establishes the phenomenon independently of it.
  Applying the identity across the wider literature requires papers to report
  ambiguous-split accuracy and bias score together, which \S\ref{sec:firstparty}
  found is not yet standard.
\item The decomposition takes the reported ambiguous score to be BBQ's official
  $s_{\text{AMB}}$ and ambiguous accuracy to be the \textsc{unknown} rate, as
  the benchmark defines them. The authors' per-item outputs are not released,
  so this could not be checked item by item.
\item BBQ conditions use one prompt template each (Appendix~\ref{app:prompts})
  with greedy decoding. The BBQ structure holds under a reworded prompt
  (\S\ref{sec:firstparty}), while absolute abstention levels are
  prompt-dependent. All \S\ref{sec:models} results use one prompt template per
  condition at temperature 0.8.
\item At 16 draws, estimated per-item power ranges from 0\% to 2\% across the
  simulated violation-size bins (Table~\ref{tab:power-magnitude}), and the
  family-wise procedure is at least as conservative. The 29 certified violations
  therefore do not provide an exhaustive count, and non-rejection does not
  establish compatibility. The null residue in Table~\ref{tab:certificate}
  measures what the certificate leaves uncertified, and more draws per item raise
  its power.
\item The generic refusal direction is not bias\hy{}specific. The
  refusal-ablated condition is the dissenting condition in 22 certified
  violations for Phi-3.5-mini and 3 for Qwen2.5, a pattern consistent with
  instrument failure. A behaviour\hy{}derived direction leaves no instrument-failure pattern among
  Qwen2.5's certified violations (one policy-effect pattern remains) and
  strengthens its recovery gap
  (\S\ref{sec:models}). It is built from the 480 gendered WinoGender items under
  the \textit{direct} template, using one greedy (temperature 0) response per
  item: items whose response parses as an abstention form one group, items whose
  response parses as an answer the other, unparsed responses fall in neither,
  and at least 8 items per group are required. The counts are items, not draws:
  58 abstentions for Qwen2.5 (422 answered) and 19 for Mistral (460 answered),
  and none for Phi-3.5-mini (480 answered) or the three OLMo-2 checkpoints
  (468--475 answered). They are not the abstention rates of \S\ref{sec:models},
  which come from 16 draws per item at temperature 0.8 and are nonzero for
  OLMo-2. On Mistral the behaviour\hy{}derived direction gives 4 patterns consistent
  with instrument failure and 1 outlier where the generic direction gave none.
  A general-purpose repair of the instrument is therefore open.
\item The abstention lean was analysed after the recovery-gap result and is
  observational. The dose reading is not tested by intervening on abstention,
  and it rests on five abstaining checkpoints from three families.
\item The \S\ref{sec:models} refusal-ablation results use WinoGender only,
  English only, one prompt template, and WinoGender carries template defects
  documented by \citet{gautam2024winopron}. The decomposition and first-party
  structure (\S\ref{sec:multilingual}) extend to six languages and twelve
  checkpoints, but on BBQ alone. The tools are benchmark-agnostic, and
  extending \S\ref{sec:models}'s instrument work to WinoGender in other
  languages, and to open-ended generation, is direct future work. Catalan is
  tested through one independently-built dataset, so a Catalan-language
  effect is not separable there from a dataset-construction effect.
\end{itemize}
