\section{Multilingual and newer-checkpoint replication}
\label{app:multilingual}

\paragraph{Panels.} Two panels are used, and they should not be mixed. The
\emph{eleven-category panel} is the 2{,}200-item English BBQ panel (200
ambiguous items per category) of \S\ref{sec:firstparty} and
Table~\ref{tab:firstparty}. Six further checkpoints, Qwen3-4B
\citep{qwen2025qwen3}, Gemma~4~E2B \citep{gemmateam2026gemma4}, Phi-4-mini
\citep{abdin2025phi4mini}, Granite-4.0-micro \citep{ibm2025granite4micro}, OLMo~3-7B
\citep{olmoteam2025olmo3}, and Ministral-3-3B \citep{mistralai2026ministral3},
ran it, giving twelve checkpoints and $12\times11=132$ cells. The
\emph{MBBQ panel} \citep{neplenbroek2024mbbq} has the same item format in
English, Spanish, Dutch, and Turkish (1{,}076 items each after matching, six
categories, so $12\times6=72$ cells per language). Its English set is a
different, smaller sample than the eleven-category panel, which is why the
English row of Table~\ref{tab:multilingual} and the English values of
Table~\ref{tab:catalan-mechanism} are labelled MBBQ and do not match
quantities computed from Table~\ref{tab:firstparty} (for Qwen2.5,
$b=s_{\text{AMB}}/(1-\text{abstention})$ is 0.63 on the eleven-category panel and
0.86 on MBBQ-English). The remaining sets are Korean (KoBBQ
\citep{jin2023kobbq}, 1{,}140 items, 108 cells) and Catalan (CaBBQ
\citep{ruizfernandez2025esbbq}, built independently rather than translated,
1{,}968 items across the 10 categories it defines, which do not coincide
exactly with BBQ's eleven). All twelve checkpoints ran every set.
Table~\ref{tab:multilingual} gives the per-language summary, and
Table~\ref{tab:catalan-mechanism} the recovered committed-answer bias behind
the Catalan exception discussed in \S\ref{sec:multilingual}. MBBQ's translated
answer-group labels for Gender were mapped to the English terms the matcher
expects (12 terms, four languages). Every other category and language use the
matcher unmodified against the benchmark's own labels, since CaBBQ's schema is
the original BBQ format and KoBBQ ships pre-matched choice lists.
